# Teacher Guide

You get a teacher account from an administrator. You see only the **subjects and classes assigned to you**.

## What you can and cannot do
| You can | You cannot |
|---|---|
| Write and edit questions (with images) for your subjects | Mark answers by hand or change any score |
| Build exam papers (pick questions, or random rules) | Upload question files (Word, Excel, PDF) |
| See analytics for your subjects and classes | See other teachers' subjects (unless assigned) |
| Export your subject reports to PDF and Excel | Delete or edit students, or see passwords |
| Mark questions "practice eligible"; switch practice on or off for your subjects | See results before they are published (during a live exam you see participation numbers only) |

All marking is automatic. That is why every question type has an answer the computer can check.

## 1. Writing questions
Question types: multiple choice (one answer), multiple choice (several answers), true/false, matching, fill in the blank (accepted answers, or a number with tolerance), ordering.
- Add **images** from camera, gallery or computer. Every image needs a short **description** (alt text) for students who use screen readers.
- Set subject, form, topic, difficulty, marks, language (English or Kiswahili), the correct answer, and an **explanation** that students see in review after results.
- Use the **live preview** to see the phone and computer view. Drafts autosave.
- Status: Draft, then Submitted, then Approved by an admin (the school may turn approval off). Only approved questions go into exams.
- Editing a question never changes exams already taken, because each exam keeps its own copy.
- A question already used in an exam is archived, not deleted.

## 2. Building an exam paper
Choose questions by hand, or set **random rules** (for example 10 easy, 20 medium and 10 hard from chosen topics). An admin sets the date, times, classes, pass mark and rules text and schedules the exam. A live exam cannot be edited.

## 3. Practice mode
Tick **practice eligible** on approved questions you are happy for students to practise (you can do many at once). Questions that sit in any exam whose results are not yet published are **never** offered, so you cannot spoil an upcoming exam. You can switch practice on or off for each of your subjects. Students need at least 5 eligible questions for their form.

## 4. After the exam: analytics (only after results are published)
- **Subject dashboard:** average, highest, lowest, pass rate, grade distribution, trend across exams, comparison of classes and streams.
- **Students:** list with score history, flags for **needs support** (low or declining) and **top performers**. Open a student for scores over time, topic strengths and weaknesses, time taken, participation, **streak and practice activity**.
- **Question analysis:** percent who answered correctly, the most chosen wrong option, difficulty. Use it to decide what to reteach and which questions to improve.
- **Topic heat map** for each class.
- **Integrity information:** how many times a student left the exam screen. This is **information only**. A phone call or a notification can cause it. Never treat it as proof of cheating.
- **Export** to PDF or Excel.

## 5. Encouraging students
Engagement numbers (streaks, practice sessions) help you notice students who need encouragement. Rewards never change marks, so please do not link them to grades. Celebrate effort and improvement.
