# Amani Online Exams: Student Guide

## 1. Getting started
1. Open the exam website on your phone or computer. Your teacher or the school office will give you the web address.
2. **Username:** your first name in small letters, with no spaces (for example `amina`). If two girls share a first name, the second one gets a number (`amina2`). The school office has a printed list.
3. **First password:** your index number. The first time you log in, you must choose a **new password**. Choose something only you know and write it down somewhere safe at home.
4. Tick **Show password** if you want to check what you typed.

> You can only be logged in on **one device** during an exam. If you log in on another phone, the first one is logged out.

## 2. Install the app on your phone (optional, but better)
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the menu (three dots) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**. You need iOS 16.4 or newer for notifications.

Installing makes the app open faster and lets us send you reminders.

## 3. Your home page
- **Upcoming exams:** cards you can swipe. They show how many days remain, then hours and minutes on exam day.
- **Streak, XP and level:** see section 7.
- **Students online:** during an exam you can see *how many* students are online. Names are never shown.
- **Practice demo:** try the exam screen before the real exam so nothing surprises you.

## 4. Before the exam
- Charge your phone, or plug it in. Check your data bundle.
- Sit somewhere quiet. Open the app a few minutes early.
- At the start time the **START** button turns on. Read the rules and tick **I have read and agree to the rules**.
- You may start up to the **late-entry time** (usually 10 minutes) after the start. If you start late, you do **not** get extra time.

### Phone settings and location (every exam)
- Before you can press START you must: turn on **Location (GPS)**, **Wi-Fi** and **Bluetooth**, allow Location for the app, tap **Check my phone**, tick the confirmation, and tick that you accept the **Terms of use** and **Privacy policy**.
- Keep Location, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on until you submit. Your position is used only to check that students sit at least 20 metres apart. If Location is switched off, your questions are hidden until you turn it on again, and the timer keeps running.
- Sit apart from other students writing the same exam.

## 5. During the exam
- One question at a time. The top of the screen shows **Question 7 of 40** and the **countdown timer**.
- Choose or write your answer, then press **Submit**. The next question appears.
- You **cannot go back** to earlier questions. If your teacher allowed skipping, a skipped question will not come back.
- **The timer is kept by the school's computer, not your phone.** Changing your phone's clock or refreshing the page gives no extra time.
- **If your phone dies or the internet drops:** log in again. You continue at the same question with the correct time left. Answers you made offline are sent when the connection returns.
- Warnings appear at 10, 5 and 1 minute before the end.
- The system records when you leave the exam screen (switch app or tab). This is only information for your teachers; a phone call can be a good reason. Copy and paste are switched off on questions.
- When time ends, your saved answers are submitted automatically.

## 6. Results
- Results appear **5 minutes after the exam closes**. You get a notification.
- You see your **score, percentage, grade, time taken**, how you compare with the **anonymous** class average, and the **XP and badges** you earned.
- **Review:** after results come out you can see each question with your answer, the correct answer and an explanation.
- **Analytics:** your strong and weak topics, your progress in each subject, and what to revise.
- You can download your own **PDF result slip**.
- Nobody else can see your results, and you cannot see theirs.

**Grades (NECTA, O-Level):** A 75-100, B 65-74, C 45-64, D 30-44, F 0-29. Your division is worked out from your best seven subjects. Until the term ends it is only a **projection**, not an official result.

## 7. XP, streaks and badges
XP, streaks and badges are **rewards for effort**. They **never change your exam marks**, and they never show anyone else's marks.

| You do this | You get |
|---|---|
| Submit an exam | 50 XP |
| Finish before time runs out | +10 XP |
| Score points | 1 XP for each 1% (up to 100) |
| Beat your previous score in the same subject | +30 XP |
| Perfect score | +100 XP |
| Review all your wrong answers | +10 XP per exam |
| Finish a practice quiz | 20 XP |
| Streak of 3, 7, 14, 30 days | +15, +50, +100, +250 XP |

**Levels:** Beginner 0, Learner 200, Achiever 500, Scholar 1,000, Star 2,000, Champion 3,500, Genius 5,000 XP. (The school can change these numbers.)

**Streak:** a day counts when you finish a practice quiz of at least 5 questions, sit an exam, or review your results. Days follow East Africa Time. You get **2 streak freezes each month**: if you miss a day, a freeze protects your streak. If a streak ends, start again today, you can do it!

**Badges** have bronze, silver and gold. Examples: First Steps, Perfect Score, Comeback Kid, On Fire (7 days) and Unstoppable (30 days), Subject Star, All-Rounder, Steady Pace, Mistake Master, Always On Time, Practice Pro. Open your **badge cabinet** to see what to aim for next.

**Practice mode:** short quizzes (5 to 20 questions) with instant feedback and explanations. They give XP and keep your streak alive in the holidays. They never count toward exam marks.

**Leaderboard (if your school turns it on):** ranks XP, never marks, only inside your class, with display names. You can **hide yourself** in your profile. Your profile also lets you choose an avatar and a display name.

## 8. Language and comfort settings
- **Language:** English or Kiswahili.
- **Themes:** Light, Dark, High Contrast, Colour-blind safe, Soft (for tired eyes).
- **Text size** 100% to 200%, **dyslexia-friendly font**, line spacing, **read aloud**, and **reduce motion**.
These stay available during the exam.

## 9. Problems?
- *"Username or password not right":* check capital letters and spaces. After several wrong tries the account locks for a few minutes. Wait, then try again.
- *Forgot your password:* ask your teacher or the school office to reset it.
- *The screen froze during an exam:* log in again. Do not panic; your time and answers are safe.
- *Power cut or a real emergency:* tell your teacher. An administrator can give extra time or allow a retake.
