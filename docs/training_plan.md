# Training Plan (Phase 3)

Aim: every student, teacher and admin can do her job on the system without help before the pilot exam.

| Session | Who | Length | Content | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Admin and office | 2-3 office staff, headteacher | 3 hours | Admin guide sections 1-4; bulk upload with a 20-student practice file; credentials sheet; reset a password; schedule a practice exam; live monitor; grant extra time | Each person uploads a file and schedules an exam alone |
| B. Teachers | All teachers | 2 hours | Teacher guide: write 5 questions with an image and alt text; build a paper; mark questions practice-eligible; read analytics from a sample exam | Each teacher has 5 approved questions in her subject |
| C. Students (per class) | One class at a time, in a computer room or with phones | 45 minutes | Login and new password; install the app; practice demo; the exam screen; what happens when a phone dies; XP, streaks, badges; accessibility settings | Every girl has logged in, changed her password and finished the practice demo |
| D. Parents' note | Parents or guardians | Letter | One page: what the exam is, that a phone and data bundle are needed, how to help (quiet place, charged phone), who to call | Letter sent home |

**Tips for trainers**
- Use the printed student guide (English and Kiswahili). Let students try, not just watch.
- Ask "what do you do if your phone switches off?" Answer: log in again, you continue at the same question.
- Stress that XP never changes marks and the leaderboard can be hidden.
- Note every question people ask; they become the FAQ.
